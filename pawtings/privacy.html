Privacy Policy

Pawtings! Hidden Dogs · Raman & Rey Games · Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Pawtings! Hidden Dogs on Steam or on Android (Google Play).

What we collect

We don’t collect any personal data. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don’t run any servers that receive information about you.

Your progress, settings and profiles are saved only on your own device (and, on Steam, in Steam Cloud if you use it). Postcards you make in the game are saved only on your device.

Google Play (Android)

Purchases. The full game is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game only whether the full game is owned.

Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and your daily challenge result to Google Play Games. The daily challenge table shows your Play Games name and result to other players. You can manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app.

These services are run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Steam (PC)

On Steam the game uses Steam features: achievements, the daily challenge leaderboard (your Steam name and result are visible to other players), Steam Cloud saves, online play with friends through Steam, and the Steam Workshop. Anything you publish to the Workshop is public and is handled by Steam.

These services are run by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We don’t collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
